## Purpose of the service

The service lets citizens report problems in the municipal area (for example a pothole, a broken street light, abandoned waste), follow how they are handled, comment on them and rate their resolution, and read the municipality's news and events.

**It is not an emergency channel**: if there is immediate danger to people or property, call the single European emergency number 112.

A report is a communication to the municipality: it is not an application under Italian Law no. 241 of 7 August 1990 and it does not replace the procedures for which the law or municipal regulations require a formal request (for example compensation claims, formal complaints, access to records).

## Reports and content sent by users

You are responsible for what you send. Describe the facts truthfully, relevantly and without insults. Do not include in photos or texts recognisable faces, vehicle number plates, names or other personal data of other people, nor health data or other special categories of data.

A report becomes public once the municipality takes it in charge, with title, category, status, date, address, position, description and public updates; your name and your email are not published and attached photos are not shown to the public. Before it is taken in charge it is visible only to you and to municipal staff. Comments are published at once, with the name of the author.

The municipality does not check content before publication, but may edit, hide or remove content that is offensive, unlawful, off-topic or contains personal data of third parties, according to the [media policy](media-policy).

By sending a report, a comment or an attachment you declare that you are entitled to do so and you allow the municipality to use, keep and publish it within the limits described above, to run the service and to account to citizens for its work.
