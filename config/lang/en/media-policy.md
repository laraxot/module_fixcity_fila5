# Media policy

This page explains how the municipality uses its online communication channels and which rules apply to those who take part: the comments and photos sent with the reporting service and the official social media profiles of the municipality listed in the site footer.

## What the channels are for

The channels are used to inform about the municipality's activities, answer questions of general interest and collect opinions and suggestions. They publish news, events, notices, updates on reports, photos and videos. They may share content from other bodies or parties when it is useful to citizens; sharing does not mean endorsement.

**They are not emergency channels**: if there is immediate danger, call 112. To report a problem, use the reporting service, so the request is recorded and followed up. Messages on social media do not replace the formal applications required by law.

## Rules for participants

Whoever comments is responsible for what they write and publish. We ask you to:

- express your opinions fairly, with moderation and respect for other people;
- stay on topic with the content or report you comment on;
- not publish personal data of yourself or others with no public relevance: phone numbers, home addresses, number plates, recognisable faces, health data;
- not publish advertising, spam, messages for private or commercial purposes or election propaganda;
- respect copyright and trademarks: publish only texts, photos and videos you hold the rights to.

## Moderation

Comments on the reporting service are published at once, without prior checks. Where the platform allows it, municipal staff hide or remove content that:

- uses offensive, threatening, vulgar or disrespectful language;
- is unlawful or incites unlawful acts, or spreads false or alarmist news;
- discloses personal data or damages other people's reputation;
- is obscene or pornographic;
- discriminates on grounds of gender, origin, religion, political opinion, sexual orientation, age or disability;
- infringes copyright or other third-party rights;
- is off-topic, advertising or repeated.

Whoever repeatedly breaks these rules, or puts the security of the service at risk, may be blocked, where possible after a first warning. Unlawful content may be reported to the platform operator and to the competent authorities.

## Photos and videos

Photos attached to reports help municipal staff understand the problem and are not shown to the public. Frame the problem, not people: avoid recognisable faces, number plates and the inside of homes. Photos taken with a smartphone may contain the GPS position and the date of the shot: if you do not want to send them, turn off location in your camera.

Photos and videos published by the municipality on its channels respect the rights of the people shown; if you recognise yourself in a piece of content and want it removed, write to the contacts given in the [legal notes](note-legali).

## Content published by the municipality

Unless stated otherwise, the content the municipality publishes can be reused under the CC BY 4.0 licence, citing the source, as explained in the [legal notes](note-legali). Each social media platform has its own terms of use and privacy notice, which apply when you use its services.

## Replies and response times

The municipality reads messages and comments on office working days and hours and replies to questions of general interest. It does not guarantee a reply to every message or set response times. Personal requests, or requests that contain personal data, must be sent through the municipality's official channels, not in public comments.

## Personal data

The personal data of those who interact with the channels is processed according to the [privacy notice](privacy).
