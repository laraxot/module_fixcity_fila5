It covers the online service for reporting problems in the municipal area.

## Purposes and legal basis of the processing

**Which data we process.**

- **Account data**: first name, last name, email address, password (stored only as a cryptographic hash), preferred language and notification preferences. If you add them to your profile: phone number and profile photo.
- **Sign in with Google**: if you choose "Sign in with Google", Google sends us your first name, last name, email address, the identifier of your Google account and the address of your profile picture. We never receive your Google password.
- **Reports**: title, description, category, address and position on the map (latitude and longitude), date and time, status of the case and the code to follow it. You choose the position on the map; if you allow it in your browser, the map can suggest your current position, which we use only to place the report.
- **Attachments**: up to 5 files (JPEG or PNG photos, PDF documents) of at most 10 MB each. On upload the system removes device metadata from photos (date, camera model and GPS coordinates of the shot): only the image is kept.
- **Comments** you write on reports, with your name.
- **Rating** from 1 to 5 that you can give to your own report once it is resolved or closed.
- **Reports you follow**, to receive updates.
- **Notifications**: messages in your personal area and, if your email address is verified and you have not turned them off, emails about report updates.
- **Consents and security logs**: date, IP address and browser with which you accepted the documents at registration; log of sign-ins (IP address, browser, date and time of sign-in and sign-out) and of the operations carried out on the service.
- **Technical browsing data**: IP address and data sent by the browser, needed to show the site and protect it.

**Purposes and legal bases.**

- Receiving, handling and solving reports, informing you of their status, publishing the reports taken in charge and measuring the quality of the service. Legal basis: performance of a task carried out in the public interest or in the exercise of official authority vested in the municipality (Article 6(1)(e) GDPR; Article 2-ter of the Italian Code), within the municipal functions set by Legislative Decree no. 267 of 18 August 2000 (Consolidated Law on Local Authorities), the communication with citizens provided for by Law no. 150 of 7 June 2000 and the right to use online public services provided for by Legislative Decree no. 82 of 7 March 2005 (Digital Administration Code).
- Creating and managing your account and providing the services of the personal area under the terms of use you accept at registration. Legal basis: Article 6(1)(b) GDPR.
- Sign in with Google, profile photo, position detected by the browser, followed reports, update emails and any optional information messages. Legal basis: your choice, that is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time by turning off the feature or writing to the DPO, without affecting the processing carried out before.
- Security of the service, proof of consent and compliance with legal obligations. Legal basis: Article 6(1)(c) and (e) and Article 32 GDPR.

We do not intentionally process special categories of data (Article 9 GDPR) or data about criminal convictions and offences (Article 10): do not include them in texts or photos.

**Cookies.** The site uses only technical cookies: the session cookie, the form security cookie (XSRF-TOKEN), the "remember me" cookie if you choose it at sign-in and the cookie that stores your choice on the cookie banner (180 days). It uses no profiling cookies and no analytics tools. Technical cookies do not require consent (Article 122 of the Italian Code; Guidelines of the Italian supervisory authority on cookies and other tracking tools of 10 June 2021); the banner informs you of their use. The details are in the [cookie policy](/en/cookie-policy). Contrast, text size and dark theme are saved only in your browser.

## Recipients of the data

- **Municipal staff** authorised and instructed to process the data (Article 29 GDPR and Article 2-quaterdecies of the Italian Code): operators, supervisors and administrators of the service, who see reports with the contact data in order to handle them.
- **Processors** appointed by the municipality by contract (Article 28 GDPR): the supplier that develops, hosts and maintains the platform and the supplier of the email delivery service. The up-to-date list can be requested from the DPO.
- **Anyone visiting the site**: once the municipality takes a report in charge, it becomes public with title, category, status, date, address, position, description and public updates. Your name, your email and the case code are **not** published; attached photos are not shown to the public. Comments are public with the name and picture of the author.
- **Third-party services, acting as independent controllers**:
  - **Google** (Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC), only if you choose to sign in with Google;
  - **Gravatar** (Automattic Inc.): if your profile has no photo, next to your comments the reader's browser requests the picture from gravatar.com, sending a code (MD5 hash) calculated from your email address;
  - **OpenStreetMap**: the map, the address search and the static image of the position are loaded by your browser from the servers of OpenStreetMap and Nominatim (OpenStreetMap Foundation) and from staticmap.openstreetmap.de, which receive your IP address and the map areas or addresses you search;
  - **Esri** (Esri Inc.), only if you choose the satellite or topographic map view;
  - **Google Fonts**: the pages open an advance connection to Google's font servers, which can let Google know your IP address.
- **Public and judicial authorities**, where the law requires it.

The data is not disclosed beyond what is described here and is not sold or passed to third parties for commercial purposes.

## Transfers to third countries

The service data is processed in the European Union. Some third-party services may transfer data to the United States (Articles 44-49 GDPR):

- **Google LLC** and **Esri Inc.** take part in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework: the transfer relies on the adequacy decision of the European Commission (Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2023/1795 of 10 July 2023; Article 45 GDPR). The list of certified organisations is on dataprivacyframework.gov.
- **Automattic Inc.** (Gravatar) states that it uses standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission (Article 46(2)(c) GDPR).
- **OpenStreetMap Foundation** is based in the United Kingdom, a country covered by an adequacy decision of the European Commission.

To avoid these transfers you can sign in with email and password, upload a profile photo (so Gravatar is not used) and use the standard map.

## Retention period

- **Account and profile**: as long as the account is active. When you ask to delete it from your personal area (Settings and privacy, «Delete your account»), the account is deactivated at once: you can no longer sign in with your password or with Google. The data is erased for good 30 days later, and only once no report is still open; it is kept longer only where needed to meet a legal obligation or to defend a right.
- **Reports, attachments, comments, ratings and case history**: for the time needed to handle the report and, after it is closed, for the period set by the municipality's records retention plan (Article 68 of Presidential Decree no. 445 of 28 December 2000; Articles 43-44 of the Digital Administration Code). Published reports stay visible on the site without identifying data.
- **Proof of consent**: for the life of the account and then for the time needed to prove the consent.
- **Sign-in and operation logs**: 12 months.
- **Notifications in the personal area**: as long as the account is active.
- **Cookies**: the session cookie is deleted when you close the browser or the session ends; the cookie banner choice lasts 180 days.

## Rights of the data subject

Under the conditions and within the limits of the GDPR, you have the right to ask the municipality for:

- access to your data (Article 15);
- correction of inaccurate or incomplete data (Article 16);
- erasure (Article 17), unless the processing is needed for a task in the public interest or for legal obligations;
- restriction of processing (Article 18);
- portability of the data you provided, for processing based on consent or contract (Article 20);
- objection to processing on grounds relating to your particular situation (Article 21);
- not being subject to decisions based solely on automated processing (Article 22);
- withdrawal of consent at any time, without affecting the processing carried out before (Article 7(3)).

In your personal area you can change your notification preferences and delete your account. To correct your name, surname or email, and for the other rights, write to the data protection officer at the contacts above. The municipality answers without delay and in any case within one month, which can be extended by two months for complex requests (Art. 12(3)).

You can also go to court (Article 79 GDPR; Article 152 of the Italian Code).

## Whether providing the data is mandatory

- To create an account you need first name, last name, email and password, or sign-in with Google: without these data you cannot register.
- To send a report you need an account, the address or the position on the map and the description of the problem: without them the municipality cannot handle it. You can always report a problem through the municipality's other channels too.
- Optional: phone, profile photo, attachments, comments, ratings, followed reports, position detected by the browser, update emails and sign-in with Google. If you do not provide them, the service still works, without those features.

## Automated decision-making

The service takes no decision based solely on automated processing, including profiling, that produces legal effects concerning you or similarly significantly affects you (Article 22 GDPR). Every report is assessed, assigned and handled by municipal staff; the initial priority and status are the same for all reports.
