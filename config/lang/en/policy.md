# Personal data protection notice

FixCity processes the data submitted with a report to receive it, assess it and
provide status updates. This may include contact details, the issue location,
the description and any attachments supplied by the user.

The data is available only to the responsible municipal offices and is kept for
the time required to manage the request and meet legal obligations. To exercise
your rights, contact the responsible office using the details published on the
portal.

By continuing, you confirm that you have read this notice and consent to the
processing of your data for managing the report.
