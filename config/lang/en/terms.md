# Terms of use and disclaimer

These terms govern the use of the municipality's online service for reporting problems. By registering you accept these terms; the full rules are in the [legal notes](note-legali) and the processing of data is described in the [privacy notice](privacy).

## Account

- You can register with first name, last name, email and password, or with your Google account.
- The account is personal: do not give it to others and keep your credentials safe. If you think someone has used them, change your password and tell the municipality.
- You can change your profile data and notification preferences in your personal area. To close your account, write to the municipality at the contact details given in the legal notes.

## Reports

- Each report must concern a real problem in the municipal area, with an address or a position on the map and a clear description.
- You can attach up to 5 files (JPEG or PNG photos, PDF documents) of at most 10 MB each.
- After sending it you receive a code to follow the case. The report becomes public, without your name, when the municipality takes it in charge.
- A report is not a formal application and does not guarantee an intervention or a response time.
- For emergencies, call 112.

## Comments, ratings and followed reports

- Comments are public with your name and your profile picture and must follow the [media policy](media-policy).
- When one of your reports is resolved or closed, you can rate it once, from 1 to 5.
- You can follow other people's reports to receive status updates. Update emails can be turned off in your personal area.

## Availability of the service

The service is provided as is. The municipality may suspend it for maintenance or security reasons and may change its features. The published information, including maps and case status, is indicative.

## Disclaimer

To the extent permitted by law, the municipality is not liable for damage arising from the use of the service or from its unavailability, nor for content sent by users or for linked third-party sites and services. The municipality may remove content that breaches these terms and suspend the accounts that breach them.

## Changes

The municipality may update these terms; the version in force is the one published on this page. Significant changes are announced on the site before they take effect.
