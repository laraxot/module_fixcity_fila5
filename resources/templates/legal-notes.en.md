## Purpose of the service

The service lets citizens report problems in the area (for example a pothole, a broken street light, abandoned waste) and follow how they are handled. **It is not an emergency channel**: if people or property are in immediate danger, call the single emergency number 112. The service does not replace procedures for which law or the municipality's rules require a formal application, except as [[the municipality states on treating reports as applications]].

## Conditions of use

You may use the service for personal, non-commercial purposes, lawfully and fairly. It is forbidden to: send false or repeated reports to disturb the service; use the service for advertising, chain messages or to collect other people's data; try to reach restricted areas, tamper with how the site works or upload harmful content (viruses, harmful code). The municipality may suspend or close an account that breaks these conditions.

## Reports and content sent by users

You are responsible for what you send. Describe the facts truthfully and without insults. Do not include in photos or texts recognisable faces, vehicle plates, names or other personal data of other people. A report may be made public (title, description, address, status and photos) after the municipality takes it on; before that it is visible only to you and the operators. The municipality may edit, hide or remove content that is offensive, unlawful, off-topic or contains third parties' personal data, without any duty of prior checking. By sending a report you grant the municipality the right to use, keep and publish it to run the service and for [[any other purposes stated by the municipality]].
