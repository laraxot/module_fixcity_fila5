## Purpose of the service

The service lets you report problems in the area to the municipality, for example a pothole, a broken street light or abandoned waste, and follow how they are handled. You can browse the list and the map of public reports without an account. To send a report or write a comment you must sign in with your account.

**It is not an emergency channel.** Every report is reviewed by an operator and handling times depend on the office in charge: the service cannot be used to ask for rescue. If people or property are in immediate danger, call the single emergency number 112.

A report informs the municipality of a problem. If the law or the municipality's rules require a formal application (istanza), submit it by the means the municipality indicates for that procedure: a report made through this service does not replace it.

## Conditions of use

Using the service is free of charge. You may use it for personal purposes, lawfully and fairly, if you accept these conditions and have read the privacy notice. When you register, give true details and keep your credentials confidential: you are responsible for what happens with your account.

It is forbidden to:

- send false or repeated reports, or reports that do not concern a problem in the area, or use the service to disturb the work of the offices;
- write unlawful, offensive, defamatory or discriminatory content, or content that infringes the rights of other people;
- use the service for advertising, chain messages or to collect other people's data;
- try to reach restricted areas, get around technical limits (such as the maximum number of submissions in a period), tamper with how the site works or upload harmful files (viruses, harmful code);
- use automated programs to send reports or comments.

If you break these conditions, the municipality may refuse to publish or may remove your content and, in serious or repeated cases, suspend or close your account. How the municipality decides and how it informs you is explained in the section "Reports and content sent by users".

## Reports and content sent by users

### What you can send

A report contains the type of problem, a title, a description, the place (address and point on the map) and, if you wish, some attachments: photos or documents in JPEG, PNG or PDF format. Comments on public reports are also content sent by users.

### What you must do

You are responsible for what you send. Describe the facts truthfully and without insults. Send only texts and photos that are yours or that you have the right to use (Law No 633 of 22 April 1941). Do not put other people's personal data in photos or texts: recognisable faces, number plates, names, home addresses, telephone numbers. A person's portrait cannot be published without their consent, except in the cases provided for by law (Articles 96 and 97 of Law No 633 of 1941). The municipality may black out the parts that contain personal data of third parties.

### When a report becomes public

Before publication, an operator of the municipality reviews every report to check that it concerns a problem in the area and that it respects these conditions. Until then the report does not appear in the list, on the map or on the public pages: you can see it, the operators can see it, and so can anyone who knows your tracking code. The code is personal: do not share it.

After the operator approves it, the type of problem, the title, the description, the address and the point on the map, the status, the date of submission and the history of updates are public. Attached photos may also be accessible to the public. Your name, your email and the other data of your account are not published in the report. The operator's review does not guarantee that what is written is true. If a report is not published, the operator writes the reason and only you can see it.

### Comments

Anyone can read the comments on public reports. To write one you must sign in with your account, and the comment shows the name of your account. Comments appear immediately, without prior review: if they break these conditions, the municipality may hide or remove them afterwards.

### How the municipality decides on content or on an account

The municipality may refuse to publish, hide or remove content that is unlawful or contrary to these conditions (for example false, offensive, unrelated to the service or containing personal data of third parties) and may suspend or close an account. Decisions are taken by people appointed by the municipality, not by automated systems. The municipality decides diligently, objectively and proportionately, taking into account the rights of everyone, including freedom of expression.

If the municipality restricts your content or your account, it writes to the email address of the account and tells you what it has decided, the facts and reasons, the provision of law or the rule of these conditions it is based on, and how you can ask for a review. To ask for a review, write to the contacts at the end of this page. You can always apply to the courts and, in the cases provided for by law, to the administrative court.

### Licence you grant to the municipality

You keep the rights in the texts and photos you send. By sending them, you grant the municipality a non-exclusive, free licence to use, reproduce, store and publish them, in order to handle your report and to account for the municipality's work (for example with statistics). The licence lasts as long as the content is published or kept in accordance with the privacy notice, and it is not a transfer of rights. You can ask for your content to be taken off publication by writing to the contacts at the end of this page: the municipality assesses the request and, unless the law requires it to be kept, removes it.

## Links to third-party sites and services

The site may link to websites of other parties, which the municipality does not control and is not responsible for: a link does not mean approval of their content. Other parties may link to this site, but without suggesting that the municipality approves of them or is affiliated with them.

Maps and address search use third-party services. To show the map, your browser downloads the images directly from the servers of the providers, normally OpenStreetMap and, if the map offers them, other views (for example satellite or topographic) from other providers: they receive your IP address and the technical data of the request. To search for an address or to turn a point on the map into an address, the text searched or the coordinates are sent to the Nominatim service of OpenStreetMap. These providers have their own terms and privacy notices, which you can find on their websites. How the municipality handles your data is explained in the privacy notice.

Map data is © OpenStreetMap contributors and is available under the Open Database License (ODbL): see [openstreetmap.org/copyright](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright).
