## Purposes and legal basis of the processing

The municipality processes your personal data only for the purposes listed below. For each one we state the data processed, the legal basis and any limits.

**Legal basis of purposes 1 to 6.** The processing is necessary for the performance of a task carried out in the public interest or in the exercise of official authority vested in the municipality (art. 6(1)(e) GDPR; art. 2-ter of Legislative Decree 196/2003, the Italian Personal Data Protection Code). The basis is set out in law: art. 13 of Legislative Decree 267/2000 gives the municipality the administrative functions concerning the population and the municipal territory, and art. 8 of Law 150/2000 entrusts the public relations office with listening to citizens and checking the quality of services. Your consent is not needed for these purposes. The boxes you tick to confirm that you have read this notice (in the report form and at registration) only show that you were able to consult it: they are not consent to the processing.

1. **Registration and management of your account.**
   - Data: first name, last name, email address, password (stored only in encrypted form), date and time of registration and, where it applies, of the verification of the email address.
   - You need an account to submit a report.
   - Sign-in with Google (optional): if you choose to sign in with Google, Google identifies you and gives the municipality your first name, last name, email address, Google account identifier and profile picture. The municipality does not receive your Google password, which remains solely with Google under its own privacy policy.

2. **Receiving and handling reports.**
   - Data: type of problem, title, description, place (address and geographic coordinates of the point you indicate), any attachments (up to 5 JPEG, PNG or PDF files, each up to 10 MB), date and time, tracking code, status and history of updates. Operators also see the contact details of your account (name, email and, if it is in your profile, phone number) to handle the case and be able to contact you.
   - Device location: while you fill in the report, your browser asks for permission to use the location of your device in order to suggest the point on the map. You can refuse and indicate the place by hand or by searching for an address. The location is saved only if you confirm it as the point of the report you submit.

3. **Publication of the report on the site.**
   - A new report is not public. A municipal operator reviews it and, if the municipality takes it on, the report appears on the map and in the public list with: type, title, description, address and map point, status, day of submission, status updates and attached images. A report rejected by the operator stays non-public.
   - Your name, last name, email, phone number and the tracking code are not published.
   - Title, description and images are visible to anyone who visits the site: do not include data about yourself or other people that you do not want to make public (see “What not to include”).

4. **Messages about the status of reports.**
   - Data: email address of the account, code and status of the report.
   - Updates always appear in the Notifications of your personal area. You also receive an email for each update only if your address is verified and you have not turned the option off in “Settings and privacy”.
   - The office may also write to the address of your account to ask for clarifications or to tell you the outcome.
   - If you choose to follow a report, the municipality keeps the link between your account and that report in order to send you the updates.

5. **Evaluation of the service.** After one of your reports is resolved you can give a rating from 1 to 5. The rating is kept together with your account and the report and is used to check the quality of the service.

6. **Security of the service, prevention of abuse and proof of acceptance.**
   - Data: IP address, type of browser and device (user agent), date, time and language, recorded in the acceptance log (box for this notice, for the terms of use and for any consent to messages, with the version of the text), in the activity log (for example account registration) and in the technical logs of the server and of the application. The IP address is also used to limit, for short periods, repeated attempts to sign in, register, look up a tracking code or submit forms.
   - Legal basis: in addition to point (e), the obligation to adopt appropriate security measures (art. 32 GDPR; art. 6(1)(c)).

7. **Reports of accessibility problems on the site.** If you use the accessibility feedback form, the municipality receives the text of your message, the page concerned and, if you give it, your email to reply to you; the message is sent to the mailbox indicated in the accessibility statement. Legal basis: the obligation to provide a feedback mechanism on accessibility (Law 4/2004; Directive (EU) 2016/2102; art. 6(1)(c)).

8. **Updates and invitations (optional).** If at registration you tick the optional box to receive updates and invitations, the municipality may use the name and email of your account to send you updates and invitations. Legal basis: your consent (art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of the processing carried out before the withdrawal, and not ticking the box has no effect on the service. Consent can be given by people aged 14 or over; below 14, the authorisation of the person with parental responsibility is required (art. 2-quinquies of Legislative Decree 196/2003).

9. **Operation of the site (technical cookies).** The site uses only technical cookies: the session identifier, the form protection code and the choice you made on the cookie banner. It does not use profiling cookies or third-party traffic analysis or advertising tools. Technical cookies do not require consent (art. 122 of Legislative Decree 196/2003). Details are in the cookie notice of the site.

### What not to include

- Do not include in the text or in the photos data about health, origin, religious or political beliefs, sex life or other special categories of data (art. 9 GDPR), or data about other people (names, phone numbers, recognisable faces, number plates, the inside of homes).
- Photographs may contain metadata, for example the date of the shot and the GPS location of the device: the service does not remove them.
- If you have already included data of this kind, write to the controller or the data protection officer and ask for it to be removed.

## Rights of the data subject

You have the right to:

- access your data and obtain a copy (art. 15 GDPR);
- have inaccurate data rectified and incomplete data completed (art. 16);
- have your data erased in the cases provided for (art. 17); the right does not apply to the extent that the processing is necessary for a legal obligation or for a task carried out in the public interest (art. 17(3)(b));
- obtain restriction of processing in the cases provided for (art. 18);
- object at any time, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to processing based on the public interest task: the municipality stops processing the data unless it demonstrates compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests or the need to defend a right in court (art. 21(1)); you may also object at any time to the sending of updates and invitations (art. 21(2) and (3));
- receive the data you have provided in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format and transmit it to another controller (data portability, art. 20), but only for processing based on consent and carried out by automated means; the right does not apply to processing for a task carried out in the public interest (art. 20(3));
- withdraw at any time your consent to updates and invitations, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal (art. 7(3)).

**How to exercise them.** Write to the controller or to the data protection officer at the contact details above, stating the right you want to exercise and the email address of your account. In “Settings and privacy” in your personal area you can see your account data and choose whether to receive update emails; other requests (copy of the data, correction, deletion) are sent to the municipality.

The municipality replies without undue delay and in any event within one month of the request. The period may be extended by two further months where the request is complex or there are many requests; in that case it tells you within the first month (art. 12(3)). Exercising your rights is free of charge, except for manifestly unfounded or excessive requests (art. 12(5)). If it has reasonable doubts about your identity, the municipality may ask you for information to confirm it (art. 12(6)). If it does not act on your request, it tells you the reasons and about the possibility of lodging a complaint (art. 12(4)).

## Complaint to the supervisory authority

If you believe that the processing of your data infringes the GDPR you have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (art. 77 GDPR), in particular in the Member State of your habitual residence, place of work or place of the alleged infringement.

In Italy the supervisory authority is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (the Italian data protection authority), Piazza Venezia 11, 00187 Rome, website [www.garanteprivacy.it](https://www.garanteprivacy.it/). The complaint is lodged in the ways indicated by the Garante: certified email (PEC) to protocollo@pec.gpdp.it (an address that accepts messages only from other PEC addresses), registered letter with return receipt or delivery by hand (arts. 140-bis and following of Legislative Decree 196/2003).

A complaint to the Garante and an action before the courts are alternative routes (art. 79 GDPR; art. 140-bis of Legislative Decree 196/2003).

## Whether providing the data is mandatory

- To create an account you must provide your first name, last name, email address and password; to submit a report you must indicate the type of problem, title, description and place. No law and no contract requires you to do so, but this data is necessary to provide the service: if you do not provide it you cannot create the account or submit the report.
- To continue in the report form you must tick the box confirming that you have read this notice (acknowledgement, not consent).
- The following are optional: attachments, the phone number when it is in your profile, the device location, signing in with Google, consent to updates and invitations and the email in the accessibility form. If you do not provide them there is no effect on the rest of the service.
- IP address, user agent, date and time are recorded automatically by the site so that it can work securely; you cannot choose them while you use it.

## Automated decision-making

The service does not take decisions based solely on automated processing that produce legal effects concerning you or similarly significantly affect you (art. 22 GDPR) and does not carry out profiling. Taking on, assigning, answering and closing a report are decided by municipal staff. The service does not use artificial intelligence systems to examine or classify reports. Only some technical security checks are automatic, such as limiting repeated attempts from the same IP address, and they do not affect the outcome of your reports.
