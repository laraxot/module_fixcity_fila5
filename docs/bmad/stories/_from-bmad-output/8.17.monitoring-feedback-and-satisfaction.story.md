# 8.17 Measure service quality with consent and feedback
## Story
As a **service owner**, I want privacy-safe analytics and feedback, so that service quality improves with evidence.
## Acceptance Criteria
1. Critical outcomes have indicator, owner, cadence and interpretation rules.
2. Optional analytics/tracking remain consent-gated through Gdpr/Statikbe; no second cookie banner exists.
3. Feedback identifies accessibility, language, privacy and quality issues and has a response owner.
4. Reports are aggregate/minimized, retain method evidence and expose limitations or missing data.
## Tasks / Subtasks
- [ ] Define outcome metrics and feedback instruments (AC: #1, #3)
- [ ] Map events to consent categories and minimization (AC: #2, #4)
- [ ] Define review cadence and remediation workflow (AC: #1, #3, #4)

## Testing
- **Approach:** event-contract, consent-state and reporting review.
- **Key scenarios:** denied/accepted analytics, feedback without account and multilingual feedback.
- **Per-AC:** verify metric ownership, gating, minimization, response and limits.
- **Fixtures / mocks:** synthetic events and aggregate report.
- **Edge cases:** withdrawal, blockers, sparse tenant data and accessibility complaint.
