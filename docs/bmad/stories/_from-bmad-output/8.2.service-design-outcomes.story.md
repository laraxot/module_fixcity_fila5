# 8.2 Define service goals and measurable outcomes

## Story

As a **service owner**, I want users, stakeholders, goals and success indicators
documented, so that FixCity improves the civic task rather than only its interface.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Citizen, PA operator, supervisor and platform-owner needs are mapped to the
   report/track/resolution journey.
2. Stakeholders and decision owners are named for each critical journey.
3. Each MUST journey has at least one measurable outcome and a collection method.
4. Metrics do not introduce analytics cookies before the existing Gdpr consent gate.

## Tasks / Subtasks

- [ ] Map actors, goals and stakeholder responsibilities (AC: #1, #2)
- [ ] Define outcome indicators and evidence owners (AC: #3)
- [ ] Connect measurement to the consent-gated analytics boundary (AC: #4)
