# 4.1 Notification worker and provider drill
As an operator, I want delivery to happen after ticket commit, so that provider outages do not lose reports.
## Acceptance criteria
1. Worker, SMTP sandbox and provider configuration are verified without secrets in logs.
2. Preference/verified-destination rules are honored.
3. Failed delivery leaves a replayable record.
