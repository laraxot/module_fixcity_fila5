# 6.8 Cookie compliance owner approval

## Story

As a **tenant owner/data-protection approver**, I want the cookie inventory,
purposes and legal copy versioned and approved, so that the technical consent
flow is not mistaken for legal authorization.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. The owner approves, per tenant and locale, controller identity, purposes,
   categories, legal basis, retention, recipients, transfers and withdrawal text.
2. The approved version is linked to the deployed Cookie Policy and release
   candidate; stale or missing content blocks production sign-off.
3. The approval record identifies approver, date, locale, document version and
   change reason without storing unnecessary personal data.
4. The release checklist explicitly states that code agents do not infer legal
   basis or approve tenant copy.

## Tasks / Subtasks

- [ ] Produce owner review checklist from actual cookie/network inventory (AC: #1)
- [ ] Version localized policy and approval metadata (AC: #2, #3)
- [ ] Add production gate for missing/stale approval (AC: #2, #4)
- [ ] Record decision and unresolved legal exceptions (AC: #3, #4)

## Testing

- **Approach:** document/schema validation plus release-gate integration test.
- **Key scenarios:** approved current version, stale version, missing locale,
  changed cookie inventory and rejected approval.
- **Per-AC verification:** AC #1 → signed review; AC #2 → gate test; AC #3 →
  metadata assertion; AC #4 → checklist audit.
- **Fixtures / mocks:** tenant approval records and versioned locale content.
- **Edge cases:** tenant override, emergency legal change and withdrawn approval.
