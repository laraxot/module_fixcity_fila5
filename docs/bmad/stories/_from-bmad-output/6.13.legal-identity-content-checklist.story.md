# 6.13 Capture owner-approved legal identity and content facts

## Story

As a **municipality owner**, I want a structured legal-content checklist, so that
terms/disclaimer pages cannot be approved while identity, contacts, copyright,
third-party links or reporting-content rules are missing.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. The checklist covers tenant identity/contact, responsible office, copyright and
   intellectual-property ownership, external links/providers, acceptable use,
   uploads/media, moderation/notice-and-removal and emergency-channel wording.
2. Every field is localized or explicitly marked language-independent and has an
   owner/status/evidence state; no example values are treated as production data.
3. The public document can show an incomplete warning naming missing facts without
   exposing internal notes or inventing legal text.
4. Tests prove that a complete approved fixture differs from a template/incomplete
   fixture for all supported publication locales.

## Tasks / Subtasks

- [ ] Map the checklist to existing tenant/CMS legal document data without adding duplicate ownership models (AC: #1, #2)
- [ ] Add validation/reporting for missing or placeholder fields (AC: #2, #3)
- [ ] Render only approved public fields and keep internal review metadata private (AC: #3)
- [ ] Add multilingual completeness tests and owner-evidence fixtures (AC: #4)

## Dev Notes

- Tenant and localization context scope content and policy; legal data must remain tenant-owned. [Source: architecture.md#Data model]
- The existing privacy assessment rejects placeholders but deliberately does not write values for the owner. [Source: laravel/Modules/Fixcity/app/Actions/AssessPrivacyPolicyAction.php]
- The external research identifies publisher identity, copyright, third-party links, UGC/media rules and service limitations as distinct content areas; these are planning inputs, not legal clauses. [Source: bmad-output/research-report.md#What the external material implies for FixCity]
- Do not reuse blanket “as-is” or liability-exclusion text from example websites without legal review. [Inference]

## Testing

- **Approach:** action/unit tests for completeness plus feature rendering tests for safe incomplete output.
- **Key scenarios:** each required field missing, placeholder text, approved complete record, locale fallback and internal metadata exclusion.
- **Per-AC verification:** AC #1 → checklist schema; AC #2 → localized fixture validation; AC #3 → rendered warning/output assertions; AC #4 → locale matrix.
- **Fixtures / mocks:** tenant legal checklist with approved and incomplete variants.
- **Edge cases:** malicious Markdown/HTML, external URL validation, empty contact channel and mixed-language fallback.
