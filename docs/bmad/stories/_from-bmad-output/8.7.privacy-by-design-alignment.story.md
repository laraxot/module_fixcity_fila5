# 8.7 Align privacy by design with the service journey

## Story

As a **citizen**, I want data use and cookie choices explained at the moment they
matter, so that the service is usable without weakening GDPR safeguards.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Data collection, visibility, retention, processors and optional processing are
   mapped for each critical journey.
2. Privacy notice, cookie policy, cookiebar, form acknowledgement and revocation
   semantics are consistent and localized.
3. Optional analytics/marketing processing is technically gated by the existing
   Gdpr consent runtime; technical cookies remain documented.
4. No anonymous cookie preference is represented as authenticated GDPR audit proof.

## Tasks / Subtasks

- [ ] Map data/processing points to journey screens (AC: #1)
- [ ] Cross-check legal and cookie streams for consistent links and semantics (AC: #2)
- [ ] Verify consent gating and anonymous/audited boundary (AC: #3, #4)
