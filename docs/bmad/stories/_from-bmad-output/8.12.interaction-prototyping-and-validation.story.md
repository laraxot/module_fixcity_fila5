# 8.12 Validate interaction flows before implementation
## Story
As a **UX and product team**, I want stories, scenarios, flows and prototypes validated before build, so that implementation solves the right problem.
## Acceptance Criteria
1. Critical citizen and PA journeys have actor, goal, preconditions, happy path, errors, completion and recovery criteria.
2. Each flow has a prototype/reference and traceability to an outcome and requirement.
3. Usability and accessibility risks are recorded before implementation, with disposition and owner.
4. A flow change identifies affected stories, content and tests.
## Tasks / Subtasks
- [ ] Normalize story and scenario format (AC: #1)
- [ ] Link prototypes to outcomes, requirements and components (AC: #2, #4)
- [ ] Record validation findings and decisions (AC: #3)

## Testing
- **Approach:** scenario review, prototype walkthrough and usability plan.
- **Key scenarios:** first report, validation recovery, private tracking and PA state change.
- **Per-AC:** inspect traceability and verify every failure/recovery path has owner.
- **Fixtures / mocks:** persona scripts and localized copy.
- **Edge cases:** keyboard-only completion, interrupted session and unavailable map/media.
