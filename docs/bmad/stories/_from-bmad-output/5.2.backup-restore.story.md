# 5.2 Candidate backup and restore
As Ops, I want a tested restore, so that a municipal incident is recoverable.
## Acceptance criteria
1. Approved candidate DB/media backup is encrypted, retained and hashed.
2. Restore succeeds in an isolated environment with ownership/activity/tenant data.
3. RPO/RTO and runbook owners are recorded.
## Verification
Non-production backup/restore drill and evidence bundle.
