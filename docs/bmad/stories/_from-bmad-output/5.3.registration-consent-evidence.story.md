# 5.3 Registration consent and legal evidence

## Story

As a **citizen**, I want to read the current privacy notice and service terms
before registering, so that each required acknowledgement is specific and
traceable to the exact published document.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The registration form has separate, unchecked-by-default controls for privacy acknowledgement, service terms acceptance and optional marketing consent.
2. Privacy and terms controls expose localized, keyboard-accessible links to the published documents before submission.
3. Submission stores treatment, document identifier, locale, version or content hash, timestamp, IP and user-agent in the existing Gdpr evidence boundary.
4. The flow rejects missing, unpublished or incomplete legal documents and never invents legal copy or legal basis.
5. Tests prove that required controls block registration, marketing remains optional, and accepted records are linked to the expected document version.

## Tasks / Subtasks

- [ ] Inventory and normalize registration fields and localized legal URLs (AC: #1, #2)
- [ ] Define the evidence payload with Gdpr/User owners and approved document metadata (AC: #3, #4)
- [ ] Implement persistence through existing Actions/models without duplicating cookie consent (AC: #3)
- [ ] Add feature and browser regression coverage for all supported locales (AC: #1, #2, #5)
