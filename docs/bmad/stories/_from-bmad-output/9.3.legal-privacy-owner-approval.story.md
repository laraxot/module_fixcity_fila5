# 9.3 Obtain legal and privacy go-live approval
## Story
As a **tenant owner/DPO**, I want the public legal and privacy surfaces verified, so that the service does not launch with placeholders or unapproved processing.
## Acceptance Criteria
1. Each enabled locale has approved privacy, cookie, terms/disclaimer, accessibility and legal-notice content with version/effective date.
2. Titolare, DPO, contacts, purposes, bases, retention, processors, rights and channels are supplied by the responsible owner and traceable to the published document.
3. Consent categories, optional processing, revocation, audit boundary and acceptance-version semantics match the Gdpr stories.
4. Missing owner input produces a release blocker and safe incomplete state; code does not invent legal values.
## Tasks / Subtasks
- [ ] Collect owner/DPO data and approvals per locale/tenant (AC: #1, #2)
- [ ] Cross-check public routes, links and consent semantics (AC: #1, #3)
- [ ] Record blockers and approval evidence (AC: #4)
## Dev Notes
- Existing legal work deliberately refuses to invent identity, basis, retention or copy. [Source: bmad-output/completion-gap-analysis.md#G-15]

## Testing
- **Approach:** localized feature tests, link crawl and owner approval review.
- **Key scenarios:** complete locale, missing locale, revoked consent and outdated version.
- **Per-AC:** verify document metadata, route, link, consent and blocker state.
- **Fixtures / mocks:** synthetic incomplete tenant plus owner-approved fixture.
- **Edge cases:** tenant override and locale fallback.
