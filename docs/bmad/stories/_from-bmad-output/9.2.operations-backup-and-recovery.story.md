# 9.2 Prove operations, backup and recovery readiness
## Story
As an **operations owner**, I want background work, mail, backups and alerts proven, so that the public service is recoverable after launch.
## Acceptance Criteria
1. Queue processing, retry, failed-job handling, SMTP sandbox delivery and notification idempotency have dated evidence.
2. Monitoring covers health, queue, mail, failed jobs, storage, database and security-relevant errors with alert owners.
3. Backup is encrypted/retained according to approved policy and restored into an isolated environment with integrity checks.
4. Runbooks cover outage, queue backlog, mail failure, restore, breach escalation and rollback.
## Tasks / Subtasks
- [ ] Execute worker/SMTP/retry/failure matrix (AC: #1)
- [ ] Verify monitoring and alert routing (AC: #2)
- [ ] Execute backup/restore drill and retention evidence (AC: #3)
- [ ] Publish runbooks and on-call ownership (AC: #4)

## Testing
- **Approach:** integration drill and isolated restore.
- **Key scenarios:** retry, poison job, SMTP outage, restore, alert and rollback.
- **Per-AC:** retain logs, hashes, timestamps and owner acknowledgement.
- **Fixtures / mocks:** sandbox mail and synthetic database.
- **Edge cases:** duplicate notification, partial restore and full disk.
