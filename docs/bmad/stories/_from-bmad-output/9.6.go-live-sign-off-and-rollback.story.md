# 9.6 Make the final go-live decision
## Story
As a **release owner**, I want one evidence index and named approvals, so that launch is a controlled go/no-go decision.
## Acceptance Criteria
1. G1–G7 and all P0 rows in the go-live register link to candidate evidence with status, owner, timestamp and limitation.
2. Open P0 blockers prevent approval; deferred P1 features have explicit product decisions and do not silently expand launch scope.
3. Product, PA, QA, Ops, accessibility and privacy/legal owners sign the candidate decision; roles without approval are not marked approved.
4. Rollback, incident contact, monitoring and first-hours support are confirmed before the launch window.
## Tasks / Subtasks
- [ ] Compile evidence index and unresolved gaps (AC: #1, #2)
- [ ] Collect named approvals and product deferrals (AC: #2, #3)
- [ ] Confirm rollback and launch support rehearsal (AC: #4)
