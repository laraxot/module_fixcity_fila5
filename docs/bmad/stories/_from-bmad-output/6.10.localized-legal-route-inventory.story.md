# 6.10 Make localized legal destinations reachable

## Story

As a **visitor in any supported language**, I want privacy, cookie, legal-notice
and accessibility links to open the correct localized page, so that consent and
public-service information are actually readable and reachable.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. The supported locales have a deterministic, documented destination for privacy,
   cookie policy, legal notice/terms and accessibility declaration.
2. Every production Sixteen header/footer/auth/cookie link uses the same localized
   destination contract; no `#`, obsolete slug, missing route name or fixed
   Italian URL remains in those legal links.
3. Feature tests cover `it`, `en` and `de`, asserting successful responses or an
   explicit owner-approved CMS fallback, with no silent 404/500.
4. The cookie policy continues to link to privacy through the localization layer
   and remains available independently of optional consent.

## Tasks / Subtasks

- [ ] Inventory current legal destinations and choose one canonical slug contract per locale (AC: #1, #2)
- [ ] Register or connect the minimum Folio/CMS destinations without adding a controller/service layer (AC: #1, #3)
- [ ] Replace inconsistent Sixteen legal links and labels with the canonical localized helper (AC: #2, #4)
- [ ] Add route/content tests for it/en/de and missing-content behavior (AC: #3)

## Dev Notes

- Public pages are localized Folio/Volt representations and URLs are lowercase localized slugs with explicit locale fallback. [Source: architecture.md#ADR-002a — HTTP resource style]
- Tenant-owned privacy content and acknowledgement semantics require owner approval; implementation must not invent legal copy or legal basis. [Source: prd.md#NFR-007]
- Localization/content is a MUST requirement and critical journeys must not expose raw translation keys. [Source: prd.md#FR-014]
- The Fixcity CMS content files and feature tests prove localized legal pages exist; the remaining issue is inconsistent links and incomplete tenant inputs. [Source: bmad-output/site-audit.md#A-02]
- Reuse `LaravelLocalization::localizeURL()` for path-based links, as already done by the cookie policy. [Source: bmad-output/decision-log.md#Cookie Policy localizzata e baseline legale tecnica]

## Testing

- **Approach:** Laravel feature route tests plus Blade link inventory and browser smoke for each locale.
- **Key scenarios:** anonymous access, locale switch, cookie policy with no consent, configured CMS content, and missing content fallback.
- **Per-AC verification:** AC #1 → route/content matrix; AC #2 → static link scan; AC #3 → localized feature tests; AC #4 → cookie-policy response and link assertion.
- **Fixtures / mocks:** tenant legal-page fixtures with it/en/de content and an explicit missing-content case.
- **Edge cases to cover:** unsupported locale, trailing slash, locale-prefixed versus default-locale URLs, and owner-approved placeholder/fallback.
