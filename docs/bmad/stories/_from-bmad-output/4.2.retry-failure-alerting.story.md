# 4.2 Notification retry and alerting
As Ops, I want retry exhaustion and queue failures visible, so that citizens are not silently abandoned.
## Acceptance criteria
1. Retry/backoff/idempotency behavior is documented and tested.
2. failed_jobs and queue-depth alerts have owners.
3. Replay does not duplicate ticket state changes.
## Verification
Job failure injection, alert capture and replay runbook.
