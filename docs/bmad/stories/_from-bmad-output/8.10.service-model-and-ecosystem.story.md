# 8.10 Map the service model and ecosystem
## Story
As a **service designer**, I want actors, touchpoints and end-to-end journeys mapped, so that FixCity is designed as a public service and not isolated screens.
## Acceptance Criteria
1. A service model identifies citizen, PA, tenant, platform and external actors with responsibilities and trust boundaries.
2. A blueprint covers discovery, reporting, tracking, PA processing, notification and closure, including frontstage/backstage dependencies.
3. Failure, assisted-digital and handoff points have an owner or follow-up story.
4. The map links to module ownership and does not claim unsupported integrations.
## Tasks / Subtasks
- [ ] Model actors and ecosystem boundaries (AC: #1, #4)
- [ ] Produce blueprint and touchpoint inventory (AC: #2)
- [ ] Record failure and assisted-service gaps (AC: #3)
## Dev Notes
- The product goal is a citizen-to-PA vertical slice, so both public and operational journeys are required. [Source: bmad-output/project-context.md#Project Goal]

## Testing
- **Approach:** artefact completeness and traceability review.
- **Key scenarios:** anonymous discovery, authenticated report, reassignment, notification failure and closure.
- **Per-AC:** walk each journey and verify actors, handoffs, dependencies and gap owner.
- **Fixtures / mocks:** one synthetic tenant and citizen/PA personas.
- **Edge cases:** unauthenticated tracking and language switch.
