# 8.14 Close reliability, transparency and security gates
## Story
As a **service owner**, I want reliability, transparency and security requirements evidenced, so that citizens can trust the service and operators can recover it.
## Acceptance Criteria
1. Privacy/security by design, informative access, processor/vendor governance and incident handling map to owners and evidence.
2. DPIA applicability, processing register and retention decisions are recorded as DPO/owner decisions, not guessed by developers.
3. Critical failures have recovery, communication and escalation paths linked to operations.
4. No personal data, secrets or unsupported legal assertions are introduced.
## Tasks / Subtasks
- [ ] Cross-reference security/privacy plans with current 4.2 requirements (AC: #1, #2)
- [ ] Record DPIA, processor and incident decisions (AC: #2, #4)
- [ ] Map failure recovery and transparency evidence (AC: #3)
