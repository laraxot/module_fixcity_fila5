# 8.6 Run user research and usability validation

## Story

As a **product team**, I want representative users to validate the critical
journeys, so that design decisions are based on observed needs and not assumptions.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Research questions cover citizen reporting/tracking and PA handling, including
   accessibility and multilingual concerns.
2. The plan identifies participant roles, consent/privacy handling, tasks, success
   criteria and evidence storage without collecting unnecessary personal data.
3. Usability findings become prioritized backlog items linked to the affected flow.
4. The candidate has a repeatable usability retest plan.

## Tasks / Subtasks

- [ ] Define research protocol and participant safeguards (AC: #1, #2)
- [ ] Execute task-based usability sessions or approved proxy evaluation (AC: #3)
- [ ] Convert findings into BMAD stories and retest protocol (AC: #3, #4)

## Dev Notes

- The guide calls for interviews, qualitative research and usability tests to
  observe how users interact with services. [Source: bmad-output/research-report.md#Designers Italia: findings relevant to FixCity]
- Research data must follow the existing privacy/Gdpr boundary; never put real
  participant data in fixtures. [Source: prd.md#NFR-007]

## Testing

- **Approach:** protocol review, moderated/unmoderated task evidence and finding
  traceability review.
- **Scenarios:** mobile, keyboard, low-confidence user, language fallback and
  failure/recovery paths.
