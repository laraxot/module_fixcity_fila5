# 8.11 Inventory content, localization and rights
## Story
As a **content owner**, I want every public content item to have lifecycle, language and rights data, so that citizens receive current usable information.
## Acceptance Criteria
1. The inventory lists pages, microcopy, media and legal surfaces with owner, status, review date and source.
2. it/en/de/es coverage is explicit; missing or stale translations are visible and never silently complete.
3. Media, documents and embeds record licence, attribution, consent/liberatoria requirement and expiry where applicable.
4. Publication, correction, archival and emergency-update responsibilities are defined.
## Tasks / Subtasks
- [ ] Build content and asset inventory schema (AC: #1, #3)
- [ ] Audit locale coverage and fallback behavior (AC: #2)
- [ ] Define editorial lifecycle and rights review (AC: #3, #4)
## Dev Notes
- FR-014 requires tenant-owned localized pages and labels; do not invent tenant legal content. [Source: bmad-output/prd.md#FR-014]

## Testing
- **Approach:** schema validation plus locale/content review.
- **Key scenarios:** complete/missing translation, expired asset licence and urgent correction.
- **Per-AC:** validate required fields and it/en/de/es samples.
- **Fixtures / mocks:** synthetic CMS page, microcopy, image and PDF.
- **Edge cases:** unsupported locale and tenant override.
