# 8.9 Define design governance and responsibilities
## Story
As a **service owner**, I want design decisions, responsibilities and approvals explicit, so that the service remains governable after delivery.
## Acceptance Criteria
1. A governance record names product, PA, accessibility, privacy/legal, content, UX and technical owners and approval boundaries.
2. Objectives, stakeholder decisions, risks and assumptions have owner, date and status.
3. Research, procurement and release decisions are recorded without treating developer approval as legal approval.
4. Change triggers identify the artefacts to refresh when a tenant, requirement or language changes.
## Tasks / Subtasks
- [ ] Create the governance/RACI record and decision cadence (AC: #1, #2)
- [ ] Map approval gates to legal, accessibility and release evidence (AC: #1, #3)
- [ ] Define change triggers and maintenance ownership (AC: #2, #4)
