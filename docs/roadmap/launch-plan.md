## Go-to-Market Strategy

### Target Market Segments

#### Primary Segment: Medium Municipalities (10K-100K citizens)
- **Pain Points**: Limited resources, manual processes, citizen pressure
- **Value Prop**: 40% efficiency gain, improved citizen satisfaction
- **Pricing**: €5K-€15K/year based on population
- **Sales Cycle**: 3-6 months

#### Secondary Segment: Large Municipalities (100K+ citizens)
- **Pain Points**: Complex workflows, multiple departments, scalability
- **Value Prop**: Unified platform, advanced analytics, enterprise features
- **Pricing**: €20K-€50K/year enterprise contracts
- **Sales Cycle**: 6-12 months

#### Tertiary Segment: Healthcare Providers
- **Pain Points**: Appointment no-shows, inefficient scheduling
- **Value Prop**: 30% reduction in no-shows, automated reminders
- **Pricing**: €500-€2K/month per facility
- **Sales Cycle**: 2-4 months

### Positioning Statement

**For** municipalities and citizens who need efficient urban issue management,  
**FixCity** is a smart city platform that provides integrated reporting, healthcare, and municipal services.  
**Unlike** traditional phone/email systems or point solutions,  
**our product** offers a unified, data-driven platform with advanced geographic intelligence.

### Competitive Landscape

| Competitor | Strengths | Weaknesses | Our Advantage |
|------------|-----------|------------|---------------|
| **Traditional Systems** | Familiar, low tech barrier | Inefficient, no tracking | Digital-first, transparent |
| **Point Solutions** | Specialized features | Siloed, no integration | Unified platform |
| **Custom Solutions** | Tailored to needs | Expensive, slow to deploy | Rapid deployment, proven |
| **International Platforms** | Brand recognition, features | Not localized, expensive | Italy-focused, cost-effective |

### Pricing Strategy

| Tier | Price | Features | Target |
|------|-------|----------|--------|
| **Starter** | €5K/year | Basic reporting, up to 1K reports/month | Small municipalities |
| **Professional** | €15K/year | All features, up to 10K reports/month, analytics | Medium municipalities |
| **Enterprise** | Custom | Unlimited, custom integrations, dedicated support | Large municipalities |
| **Healthcare** | €500-€2K/month | Appointment management, medical records | Clinics, hospitals |

**Pricing Tactics**:
- Early adopter discount: 20% off first year (first 10 customers)
- Multi-year discount: 15% off for 3-year contracts
- Bundle discount: 10% off when combining multiple modules

## Risk Management

### Launch Risks

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation | Owner |
|------|-------------|--------|------------|-------|
| **Critical bug discovered** | Medium | High | Hotfix procedure, rollback plan | Tech Lead |
| **Performance degradation** | Medium | High | Auto-scaling, caching | DevOps |
| **Low adoption** | Medium | High | Enhanced onboarding, incentives | CS |
| **Negative press** | Low | High | PR crisis plan, monitoring | PR |
| **Competitor response** | High | Medium | Differentiation, speed | PM |
| **Key person unavailable** | Low | Medium | Backup resources, documentation | Launch Owner |

### Contingency Plans

#### Scenario 1: Critical Bug on Launch Day
**Trigger**: Severity 1 bug discovered during deployment

**Actions**:
1. Pause deployment immediately
2. Assess bug impact and fix time
3. Communicate delay to stakeholders
4. Fix and re-test
5. Reschedule launch (within 48 hours if possible)

**Communication**:
- Internal: Slack #launch-alerts, email
- External: "Scheduled maintenance" notice if public

#### Scenario 2: Performance Issues Post-Launch
**Trigger**: Response time >500ms for >30 minutes

**Actions**:
1. Enable maintenance mode if critical
2. Scale infrastructure horizontally
3. Enable aggressive caching
4. Disable non-critical features
5. Investigate root cause

**Communication**:
- Status page update
- Customer email if >1 hour

#### Scenario 3: Low Beta Feedback Scores
**Trigger**: Beta CSAT <4.0/5.0

**Actions**:
1. Conduct emergency user interviews
2. Prioritize quick-win improvements
3. Consider launch delay if critical
4. Enhanced onboarding and training
5. Dedicated support during launch
