## Methodology

### Research Methods Used

#### 1. In-Depth Interviews (IDI)
**Purpose**: Deep understanding of user needs, motivations, behaviors

**Format**: 45-60 minute video calls or in-person

**Participants**: 85 users across all segments

**Analysis**: Thematic analysis, affinity mapping

#### 2. Surveys
**Purpose**: Quantitative validation, statistical significance

**Format**: Online survey (15-20 minutes)

**Respondents**: 1,250 completed responses

**Analysis**: Statistical analysis, segmentation, correlation

#### 3. Usability Testing
**Purpose**: Identify usability issues, validate designs

**Format**: Moderated remote testing (Think aloud protocol)

**Participants**: 45 users, 5-8 per feature

**Analysis**: Success rate, time on task, error rate, SUS scores

#### 4. Field Studies (Contextual Inquiry)
**Purpose**: Understand real-world context, workflows

**Format**: In-person observation (2-4 hours)

**Participants**: 12 municipalities, healthcare facilities

**Analysis**: Workflow mapping, pain point identification

#### 5. Diary Studies
**Purpose**: Longitudinal understanding of behaviors

**Format**: 2-week diary entries via mobile app

**Participants**: 30 citizens

**Analysis**: Pattern identification, journey mapping

#### 6. Card Sorting
**Purpose**: Information architecture validation

**Format**: Open and closed card sorting

**Participants**: 40 users

**Analysis**: Dendrograms, similarity matrices

## User Interviews

### Interview Summary

| Phase | Dates | Participants | Format | Key Findings |
|-------|-------|--------------|--------|--------------|
| **Discovery** | Oct-Nov 2025 | 35 citizens | Remote video | Identified top 10 pain points |
| **Validation** | Dec 2025 | 25 citizens | In-person | Validated feature priorities |
| **Beta Feedback** | Jan 2026 | 15 municipalities | Remote video | Identified onboarding issues |
| **Post-Launch** | Feb 2026 | 10 citizens | Phone | Measured satisfaction |

### Key Interview Insights

#### Insight 1: Speed is Paramount
**Finding**: Citizens expect to report issues in under 2 minutes

**Quote**:
> "If it takes more than a couple minutes, I'll just forget about it." — Marco, 35

**Implication**: Optimize report flow for speed (target: <2 min)

**Action**: ✅ Implemented one-page report form with auto-location

---

#### Insight 2: Transparency Builds Trust
**Finding**: Citizens lose trust when they can't track issue status

**Quote**:
> "I reported a pothole 3 months ago. No idea if they received it or fixed it." — Elena, 38

**Implication**: Real-time status tracking is critical

**Action**: ✅ Implemented status timeline with notifications

---

#### Insight 3: Photos Are Essential
**Finding**: 85% of citizens want to upload photos with reports

**Quote**:
> "A picture is worth a thousand words. Show them exactly what's wrong." — Luca, 45

**Implication**: Photo upload must be seamless

**Action**: ✅ Implemented multi-photo upload with preview

---

#### Insight 4: Municipalities Need Metrics
**Finding**: Administrators struggle to measure performance

**Quote**:
> "The mayor asks for monthly reports. I spend 2 days compiling Excel sheets." — Giulia, 42

**Implication**: Automated reporting is high value

**Action**: 🔄 Building analytics dashboard (Q2 2026)

---

#### Insight 5: Seniors Need Assistance
**Finding**: 60% of seniors (65+) need help with digital services

**Quote**:
> "My grandson helps me, but I wish I could do it myself." — Giuseppe, 72

**Implication**: Accessibility and assisted digital support needed

**Action**: ✅ Implemented accessibility mode, phone support

---

#### Insight 6: Healthcare No-Shows Are Costly
**Finding**: 20% no-show rate costs clinics thousands per month

**Quote**:
> "Every no-show is €50 lost. It adds up quickly." — Dr. Rossi, 50

**Implication**: Automated reminders have high ROI

**Action**: ✅ Implemented SMS/email reminders

---

#### Insight 7: Mobile-First Is Non-Negotiable
**Finding**: 75% of citizens prefer mobile over desktop

**Quote**:
> "I'm never at my computer. Everything is on my phone." — Elena, 38

**Implication**: Mobile experience must be excellent

**Action**: 📋 Mobile app planned (Q1 2027)

---

#### Insight 8: Integration Matters
**Finding**: Citizens frustrated by multiple logins and systems

**Quote**:
> "Why do I need separate accounts for everything? Just let me use SPID!" — Marco, 35

**Implication**: Single sign-on (SPID/CIE) critical

**Action**: ✅ Implemented SPID/CIE integration

**Opportunity**: Unified inbox, prioritization

**Observation**: 80% of requests still on paper

**Impact**: Data entry bottleneck, errors

**Opportunity**: Digital-first, scan paper backups

**Observation**: Workers in vehicles, no desktop access

**Impact**: Delayed updates, phone calls for status
