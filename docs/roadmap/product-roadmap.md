## Executive Summary

**FixCity** is the core application module for the FixCity platform, enabling citizens to report urban issues, access healthcare services, and interact with municipality services. This roadmap outlines the strategic development plan from Q1 2026 through Q4 2027.

### Key Highlights
- ✅ **75% Complete** - Core features delivered
- 🎯 **Healthcare Management** - 100% complete
- 🎯 **Municipality Services** - 100% complete
- 🎯 **Citizen Reporting** - 100% complete
- 🔄 **Geographic Features** - 80% complete (enhancement in progress)
- 📊 **Analytics Dashboard** - Planned Q2 2026

---

## Product Vision

### Long-term Vision (2027)
**Become the leading platform for smart city management and citizen engagement in Italy and Europe.**

### Strategic Pillars
1. **Citizen-Centric Design** - Intuitive, accessible, multilingual
2. **Data-Driven Decisions** - Analytics, insights, predictive capabilities
3. **Seamless Integration** - Healthcare, municipality, third-party services
4. **Scalable Architecture** - Multi-tenant, high-performance, secure

### Product Mission
Empower citizens and municipalities with tools to report, track, and resolve urban issues efficiently while providing integrated healthcare and administrative services.

   - Staging deployment
   - Production deployment
   - Monitoring & rollback plan

## Risk Management

### Risk Register

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation Strategy | Owner |
|------|-------------|--------|---------------------|-------|
| Geographic API rate limits | Medium | High | Implement caching, fallback providers | Tech Lead |
| Data privacy compliance | Low | Critical | Regular audits, DPO review | PM |
| Performance degradation at scale | Medium | High | Load testing, optimization sprints | Tech Lead |
| AI model accuracy issues | High | Medium | Human-in-the-loop, continuous training | Data Engineer |
| Third-party service downtime | Medium | Medium | Redundancy, circuit breakers | DevOps |

### Risk Mitigation Actions

1. **Geographic Services**
   - ✅ Implement Redis caching for geocoding results
   - ✅ Multi-provider fallback (Google Maps + OpenStreetMap)
   - 🔄 Rate limit monitoring and alerting

2. **Data Privacy**
   - ✅ GDPR compliance audit completed
   - 🔄 Data retention policies implementation
   - 📋 Regular privacy impact assessments

3. **Performance**
   - 🔄 Load testing infrastructure setup
   - 📋 Monthly performance benchmarks
   - 📋 Optimization backlog
